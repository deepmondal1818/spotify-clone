## Where to Find Free MP3s

### Free Royalty-Free Music Sites:
- **Pixabay** (pixabay.com/music) - High quality, royalty-free music
- **Freepd** (freepd.com) - Free background music
- **Incompetech** (incompetech.com) - Royalty-free music library
- **YouTube Audio Library** - Login to YouTube Studio for free music
- **Zapsplat** (zapsplat.com) - Royalty-free music and sound effects
- **Bensound** (bensound.com) - Royalty-free background music

### Steps:
1. Find and download the MP3 files from one of the sites above
2. Place them in this folder (`public/audio/`)
3. Name them exactly as listed above
4. Refresh your browser and start playing!

The player will automatically use these files when you click play.
